Reversal pre-alpha for Apple Silicon Macs, macOS 13 or later.

Install
1. Drag Reversal to Applications.
2. Open it. macOS says it can't verify the developer, because this pre-alpha isn't
   notarized by Apple. Choose Done, not Move to Trash.
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and choose
   Open Anyway next to Reversal. Confirm with your password.

You only need to do this once for each version.

Use
Choose a folder of raw scans, or drop one on the window. Reversal saves DNGs to a
"positives" folder inside it and never changes your scans.
It doesn't connect to the internet: its window talks only to a program on your Mac.

Known limits
Tested with Nikon D5100 scans of color negative film. Other cameras should open, but
color was fitted to this camera. DNGs with lossy JPEG compression can't be read.
