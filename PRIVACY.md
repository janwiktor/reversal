# Privacy

Reversal collects no data. The command line, the web UI, the Mac app and the iPhone and iPad app
all work on your own device: they have no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and
they don't connect to the internet. Your scans and the DNGs made from them stay where you keep
them.

On iPhone and iPad:

- **Files** you open, copy in or have another app open in Reversal are read on the device. Rolls
  copied into the app stay in its own folder until you delete them.
- **Photos:** raw photos you choose reach the app through the system's photo picker, which hands
  over only what you chose. Reversal asks for permission to add to your library only to save
  DNGs there, when you choose to.
- **Exports** go only where you send them, through the system's share sheet.

If you test Reversal through TestFlight, Apple gives the developer the feedback and screenshots
you choose to send, and crash reports from your device if you allow it. Apple's
[TestFlight privacy terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/) cover these.

Questions about privacy go to [GitHub issues](https://github.com/janwiktor/reversal/issues).
